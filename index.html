Real threads

Real threads, ready for 3D printing, in a few clicks

A real helical thread you can print, not a texture — in a finished hole or on a shaft: pick the round face and a size, or let Auto match the hole. ISO metric, UNC / UNF, G, Trapezoidal, Print-friendly with flat crests, or Custom — your own pitch, flank angle and depth for jars, lids and anything non-standard. A print clearance of any size (0.2 mm by default) lets printed parts screw together.

AI × 3D printing

AI optimization for 3D printing, with a minimum of supports

Ask your AI to prepare the part for printing: it can turn overhangs into 45° chamfers, give the part a flat base and split what cannot print in one piece. Every change is an ordinary feature in the history — check it, edit it or undo it.

3D sketch

3D lines in a 3D sketch, on a virtual cube

A very easy way to draw in space: a cube of virtual points around the origin, its size and step are yours. The line runs in all three directions: pick points on any layer — XY, XZ, YZ — or in the whole Cube; Tab / Shift+Tab steps the layer, points drag with the mouse. Lines, Spline or Through points — the line becomes a path for Sweep and Loft.

Photo → real size

One photo and one measurement — real dimensions

Insert a photo, click two points and type one real length: the whole picture scales, and you design over it in true millimeters. Photo taken at an angle? Horizontal or Vertical turns it so that line is level.

Emboss · Engrave

Text and any sketch — raised or engraved on any face

Emboss raises a Text or any closed sketch — letters, a logo, a star — and Engrave cuts it in: on flat faces, wrapped round cylinders and cones, projected onto spheres. Letters that run past a face carry on round onto the faces that continue it smoothly, so mixed surfaces work too. Any installed font, several lines, Cyrillic, text along a curve; Evenly round spreads a word all the way round.

Gears · Worms

Gears and worm drives in one dialog

Spur Gears makes a meshing pair at once, no sketch: involute teeth, straight or helical (5–45°), sized by teeth, ratio or center distance, with bores for the shafts. Worm Gear makes the worm and its wheel, 1–6 starts. A third gear meshes with one already there; Make Assembly puts every gear into a part of its own.

Pattern

Patterns: spread evenly between two faces, skip the copies you don't need

Equal spacing spreads the copies evenly between two limits — two faces, edges or points — so you never count the step yourself. Excluded: click the copies you don't need right on the model and they are left out; ✕ brings one back. Rectangular, circular and Mirror for features; in sketches also Along Path — copies placed along any curve: lines, arcs, splines.

Split for printing

Too big for the printer? Split it — with a dovetail

Draw the cut line in a sketch — straight, an arc or a dovetail — and Split ▸ Keep all cuts the part along it, every piece a Solid of its own. Any clearance you need: draw the line a second time with Offset by the gap — the thin strip between the lines becomes a Solid too; delete it, and the gap is there. Then Make Assembly turns the pieces straight into an assembly, each a part ready to print; the dovetail holds the printed pieces together.

Drawings, a new Smooth for any part, simple AI connection, thread profiles

Added

  • A very basic and simple drawing of a part or an assembly: views, scale, dimensions, text, title block, pen and marker, PDF. The open drawing follows its part.

It runs on your computer and takes time — minutes for a simple part, an hour or more for a complex one: run it overnight, or use it on simple parts. A connected AI is there to speed this up too: it rebuilds the part by the same method, much faster.

  • Threads: DIN 405 round and DIN 513 buttress; Custom profiles — triangle, trapezoid, semicircle; cut into the surface or grow onto it; Save for the next part.
  • A start page with your recent files and their pictures; Return to Assembly on a part opened with Edit; Split ▸ Make Assembly.

Fixed

  • Extrude To a face of the part from a sketch on a work plane joins the part (it made a separate solid).
  • The drawing sheet no longer jumps while you dimension; the grounded part of an assembly is marked on the part itself.

How it works

Familiar to anyone who has used a parametric CAD

Lines, arcs, splines and text; dimensions typed while you draw; a Constraint List; sketch patterns.

Features

STL → STEP

STL into STEP with AI

An STL is a mesh of triangles: a slicer prints it, but you cannot change a size or open it as a solid in another CAD. Connect your AI, and it rebuilds the STL in ProstoCAD as a real part — sketches, Extrude, Revolve, sizes you can edit — ready to save as STEP. Or do it yourself with the STL tools below.

How to connect, step by step

01Import

The STL comes in as it is; the status line counts its triangles.

02STL → STEP

The mesh becomes a solid of its triangles, ready to export as STEP.

03Mesh Section

Cut the STL by a plane: a clean sketch — sharp corners, lines, arcs, one smooth spline where the shape is free.

04Smooth

Any part rebuilt as features: Revolves for a turned one, Extrudes of its steps for a plate or a box — the nearer kept. Takes time on your computer: best overnight, or on simple parts. A connected AI does it faster.

AI · MCP

Connect your AI — step by step

ProstoCAD has an MCP server built in (AI Link): nothing to download for it. Any AI program that speaks MCP can work in your ProstoCAD window — look at the part, measure an STL, build features you can edit.

AThe quick way: Connect AI

  1. In ProstoCAD, open the part or import the STL.
  2. Tools ▸ AI ▸ Connect AI — or its arrow, to choose Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI. Installed: it starts at once, connected to ProstoCAD, in the part's folder. Not yet: a Terminal (on Windows a command prompt) installs it — Node.js too, when it is missing — connects it and starts it. Sign in there once with your own account.
  3. Tell the AI what you want, in your words:
    “Rebuild the STL in ProstoCAD as a part, sharp edges first.” “Optimize this part for 3D printing with as few supports as possible.” “Put an M6 thread in the hole on the top face.”

Gemini CLI no longer takes a personal Google sign-in: choose Use Gemini API Key and paste a free key from aistudio.google.com/apikey (Create API key). ProstoCAD keeps it for you: How to Connect ▸ Gemini API key ▸ Save key.

BAny other MCP program

The easy way: Connect AI ▸ arrow ▸ AI Apps ▸ Add to AI apps — ProstoCAD is written into the MCP settings of every one found on your computer (Antigravity, Gemini CLI, Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, LM Studio); restart the program. By hand: add a local (stdio) MCP server named prostocad in the program's MCP settings:

Safe and under your control

  • AI Link (on by default) listens only on this computer and only to programs that know the key ProstoCAD keeps in its settings folder.
  • Switch it off in Tools ▸ AI ▸ AI Link, and no AI can reach ProstoCAD.
  • The AI program and its account are your own choice.
  • Ask for the plain shape first, fillets and chamfers after — easier to check. Tried with Claude; small local models lose track of sizes and planes.
  • Work on copies and check what the AI built before you trust a size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is ProstoCAD free?

Yes. Download it and use it for free.

Do I need FreeCAD installed?

No. The FreeCAD 1.1.3 engine is inside ProstoCAD. It keeps its own settings and does not touch an installed FreeCAD.

macOS says it cannot verify the app

The test build is not notarized by Apple. Click Done (not Move to Trash), open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll to “ProstoCAD was blocked…” and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password. Needed only once.

On macOS 13 and 14 a right-click on the app ▸ Open does the same. Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ProstoCAD.app

Does it run on Intel Macs or Linux?

Linux: a version is coming soon. Intel Macs: not in this build. Today ProstoCAD runs on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer, macOS 13 or newer) and on Windows 10 / 11 x64.

Which file formats does it read and write?

Opens FreeCAD files (.FCStd), STEP and STL; imports IGES, OBJ and JPG / PNG / BMP pictures to sketch over; exports STEP (exact solids) and STL (with a precision slider for slicers).

Can FreeCAD open my files?

Yes. A part is a FreeCAD PartDesign body, a sketch a Sketcher sketch, an assembly uses FreeCAD's own joints: plain .FCStd files. ProstoCAD opens files made in FreeCAD too.

What does the AI need?

An AI command-line tool signed in with your own account: Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI (Codex and Gemini CLI need Node.js). Connect AI shows where to get them. AI Link listens only on your computer and only to programs with the ProstoCAD key; switch it off and no AI can reach ProstoCAD.

Something went wrong. How do I report it?

Chapter 11 of the manual says where the log is and what to send; then write to us — see Contact.

A one-person project
